A private dashboard for one person's bank accounts

FinKit reads the owner's own bank transactions, sorts them into categories, and prepares the monthly figures for a budget spreadsheet. It is a personal tool, not a service for the public.

Sign in Privacy Terms

Read-only

It can see account names, balances and transactions. It cannot make payments or move money.

Kept private

Transactions are shown in the owner's browser and are not kept on the server. Bank logins never reach FinKit.

Owner access only

Signing in is limited to the owner. There are no public accounts or sign-ups.
