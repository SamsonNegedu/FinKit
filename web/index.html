FinKit Sign in

A private, self-hosted dashboard for your bank accounts

FinKit runs on infrastructure you control. It reads your transactions (file import or optional bank sync), categorizes them in your browser, and can prepare monthly figures for a spreadsheet you own. No FinKit cloud, no telemetry, and no data sold or shared, unless you turn on optional Google Sheets or bank sync yourself.

Sign in Privacy Terms

Self-hosted

Web app and API run on your machine or server. SQLite rules and tokens stay on your disk; transactions stay in your browser unless you export.

No data gathering

No analytics, ads, or crash reports built into FinKit. Nothing is sent to the software authors by default.

Read-only banking

With sync enabled, it can fetch accounts and transactions. It cannot move money. Bank passwords never reach FinKit.

Optional export only

Transactions are not uploaded elsewhere unless you configure Google Sheets export or open-banking sync.
